**Tally:** 20 better, 4 the same, none worse. The mean goes from 5.0 to 6.8. The top 12 average +0.75 and the bottom 12 +2.8.

## Patterns

The full rethinks of the bottom 12 made the biggest jumps, but that's partly arithmetic: going from a coin to a picture is +3 for free. Most of them land at 5 or 6, a usable set scene rather than a peak-hour one. The one real exception is **Flyover**, which went from bottom-12 to a genuine 8 by borrowing the "drop is a place" idea from Expressway. Among the top 12, the small, targeted tweaks that fixed one named room problem worked best: Thresholds' sepia drop, Murmuration's missing white-out, Current's dye and Sumi's night ground. The sideways moves came from redesigners changing the picture itself (Chladni's tilt, Scanlines' drop), which traded one strength for another. The recurring failure is that **the redesigns of old sketches went cream**: Planets, Rings, Text, Jags and Grunge all fixed legibility by moving onto a bright paper ground. That's the exact trap I called out in round one, just moved to new scenes.

## Raph's question: is there tangible aesthetic improvement?

**Yes: tangible, broad and mostly in the right direction.** Nothing got worse, the dead scenes are now alive, and three V2s (Chomper, Thresholds, Flyover) are the best versions of anything in the collection.

- **The one I'd project at a party:** **Chomper V2.** The tilt before the drop, the fold, and the porthole dive into a fresh-coloured maze every breakdown. The room will cheer the first fold and try to guess the next colour by the third.
- **The one I'd send back:** **Rings V2.** It solved "tiny" by becoming a full-frame cream op-art field that churns entirely on the drop. That's a floodlight plus a shimmer hazard in a dark room. Put the same rings on a dark ground and it could be a 7. (Interference V2 gets an honourable mention: it didn't get worse, but it didn't visibly fix the aliasing either.)

## What the redesigns lost

- **Chladni** lost the clean top-down figures. The tilt makes the far half of the plate unreadable, and the nodal pattern was the whole show.
- **Scanlines** lost its V1 rolling kick ring in the drop. It's there in the build, but in the green range it's absorbed into ridge highlights. That ring was the collection's model confined kick; give it back its own colour in the drop.
- **Murmuration** lost the violet-and-pink dusk range. Every drop is now orange, which is prettier per frame but less varied over an hour.
- **Sumi**'s 96 s render is on cream paper. If that's a stale render, fine. If it's what the scene falls back to, it undoes its biggest improvement.
- **Current** is no longer strictly one ink. The dye is worth it, but the single-hue purity was part of why it sat so well in a dark room. Keep the dye brief.
- **Text, Planets and Rings** moved from black grounds to cream. Their V1s were bad, but they were dark.
