## Patterns

The full rethinks of the bottom 12 produced the big gains. Arcs, Rings, Text, Jags, Planets and Flyover all went from sketch to composed page, largely by moving from light on black to ink on paper, with limited palettes and full-bleed layouts. The small tweaks to the top 12 mostly came out Same or +1. That is the right outcome for work that was already good, although several changes (Koi, Zen, Interference) are too small to see from across a room. Two top-12 redesigns overreached: Sumi and Low Poly both traded a clear palette for murk. Restraint was the most reliable lever. Removing bloom, rainbow and glow (Murmuration, Physarum, Chladni, Current) never hurt a piece.

## Raph's question

**Yes, tangibly.** 18 of 24 V2s are Better, 5 are Same and 1 is Worse. The bottom of the collection went from embarrassing to respectable, and several pieces moved into the top tier.

- **Most want projected at a party:** Planets V2. It is a printed orrery that keeps moving, has a point of view and would hold a room.
- **Send back:** Sumi V2. Restore the paper ground and keep the drop's karst pillars.

## What the redesigns lost

- **Sumi** lost its paper, the very thing that made it ink painting.
- **Grunge** lost its layered, half-legible density. It is now tidy, which Carson never was.
- **Thresholds** lost its strict black-and-cream engraving during the drop.
- **Twinkle Toph** lost V1's two-ink stipple portrait in favour of generic starfield navy.
- **Low Poly** lost V1's warm dusk palette.
