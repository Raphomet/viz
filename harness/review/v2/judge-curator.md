## Patterns

Restraint in the brief produced restraint on screen. The redesigns that improved most (Physarum, Chladni, Current, Murmuration) did one thing: they removed a second idea, whether a colour, a species or a glow, and let the first idea hold the frame. Among the top twelve, full rethinks were rare and did not need to happen. The losses (Attractor, Zen) came from adding a system on top of a finished piece, such as a 3D volume or extra rakers, where a light edit would have done. Among the bottom twelve every redesign improved, by about 2.7 points on average, mostly for a boring reason: the V1s were drawn at a tenth of the size they needed, and scaling them to the stage fixed half the problem. The rethinks that also cut (Flyover, Parametric Lines, Grunge's one word at a time) climbed furthest. The ones that filled the new space with wallpaper (Arcs, Jags, Rings in their drops) climbed less. The drop is still where the collection loses its nerve: almost every V2 drop is fuller than it needs to be.

## Raph's question

Yes. The improvement is tangible and mostly lives in the bottom twelve: every one got better, and four of them (Flyover, Parametric Lines, Rings, Jags) became scenes worth keeping. The top twelve moved much less. Of the eleven I can call from the default render, five are better by a point or two, four are the same and two are worse; Sumi is better on paper but not as shipped on silk. That is the honest result of redesigning work that was already good.

- **Most want projected at a party:** Chladni V2. Sand on slate in raking light, a kick that travels, figures that stay legible through the drop. One material, done perfectly.
- **Send back:** Zen V2. Return to one rake with a job. Keep V2's new rule that rings persist until the rake passes, and keep the lower sun, but drop the crew and the seams.

## What the redesigns lost

- **Attractor:** the fine, countable threads of a single long exposure. It has more volume now and less line.
- **Zen:** the rake as a single character. It is now a crew.
- **Sumi:** the paper, as a default. It is still there as an option, and should be the default.
- **Thresholds:** the clean inversion, dark ground to cream. The ochre drop is warmer but less decisive.
- **Arcs:** the lone crescent, the purest gesture in the original sketches.
- **Jags:** the quiet. V1's hairlines on parchment were an Agnes Martin waiting to happen; V2 chose Riley instead, and Interference already does that.
