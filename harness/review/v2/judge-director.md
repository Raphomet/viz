## Patterns

The bottom-12 rethinks are where the gains are, and they're large: every one improved, most by four to six points. The trick that did it was always the same one, *stage the existing idea* (give it the whole frame, a build signal, a drop event and an exit) rather than invent a new one. Flyover, Planets and Rings each borrowed a set-piece structure (the gorge, the flyby, the rolling ring) and immediately became films instead of meters. At the top, small tweaks that *added a staged event* worked (Zen's rake crew, Current's dye, Chomper's dive, Scanlines' three acts). Tweaks that *removed something for taste* tended to cost me the thing I watch for: Physarum's antagonist, Chladni's bright spray, Murmuration's legible turn and Sumi's paper all went for good reasons (less glow, less prism) and took some of the storytelling with them. Restraint is right, but a subtraction needs an event of its own to replace what it took away.

## Raph's question

Yes, tangibly, and overwhelmingly so at the bottom. The mean rise across the 24 is about two points. The top 12 are roughly flat: five improved, four slipped a point and three held. The one I'd most want projected at a party is **Chomper V2**, whose fold-then-dive gives every drop a new world and every breakdown a way out. The one I'd send back is **Physarum V2**: the organism is prettier but the drop no longer has a second character, so it has become an effect again.

## What redesigns lost

- **Physarum:** the invading magenta species, the only drop in the set with an antagonist.
- **Chladni:** the bright, local sand spray. The kick is now a faint ring you have to hunt for.
- **Murmuration:** the iridescent bank that made the clap's turn visible across a room.
- **Sumi:** the cream handscroll paper in the default (it survives as an option). At night it's another dark scene in a collection trying to escape dark scenes.
- **Low Poly:** V1's warm pink-and-gold dusk palette, the one thing V1 had going for it.
- **Thresholds:** a little of the "door opens onto daylight" release, traded for a room-friendly sepia.
