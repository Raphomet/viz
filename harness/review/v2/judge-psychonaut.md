## Patterns

The small, surgical redesigns of strong scenes gave the best gains per unit of change. Thresholds, Scanlines, Current and Murmuration each fixed one named weakness (two-state longevity, the single hue, the cheap rainbow flock) and kept everything else, and each gained a point. The biggest jumps came from full rethinks of the weakest sketches, and the winners there all found **depth or a tunnel**: Flyover's river valley, Rings' bore, Toph's constellation. Rethinks that stayed on a flat, bright, reading surface (Text, Grunge, Dot Matrix, Arcs, Vorticism) gained points but still can't hold a high viewer. The one outright loss came from **subtracting** a layer for purity's sake (Physarum's second species), and Chladni's draining of violet was a near miss of the same kind. The lesson I'd take: the redesigns that went wrong for my audience were cautious ones, not bold ones.

## Raph's question

**Yes, tangibly.** 17 of 24 improved on my scale, 6 held, 1 got worse. The top scenes gained without losing their souls, and the bottom twelve rose by an average of nearly 3 points (2.7). But the gains are uneven. The best six V1s moved from 7–8 to 8–9, while most of the bottom twelve moved from unwatchable to decent, and only Flyover and Rings crossed into something I'd leave on.

- **Project at a party:** Scanlines V2, with Thresholds V2 a hair behind. It is a flight over a landscape that becomes a new world, in a new light, every section, for as long as the music runs.
- **Send back:** Physarum V2. It removed the thing that made the drop hypnotic, and the fix is simply to give the second species back, perhaps in a matte ink rather than glowing magenta.

## What the redesigns lost

- **Physarum** lost its two intertwined species, the braid that was its drop.
- **Chladni** lost the violet plate that gave the sand something to glow against; it also dropped the drifting motes.
- **Sumi** lost the warm paper and its tactile grain (Raph's own call for dark rooms, and a fair one).
- **Murmuration** lost the V1 violet-and-pink sky and the long sun rays in the intro; the V2 intro is greyer.
- **Scanlines** traded the kick-ring pools I loved at 12 and 60 s in V1 for ridges lit on the kick; there are fewer visible rings on the 24 s sheet.
- **Zen**'s calm lost some serenity to the extra rakes in the drop.
