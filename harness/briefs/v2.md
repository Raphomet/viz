# V2 redesigns

Raph (2026-09-28), after the six-judge panel (`harness/review/`): take the
consensus top 12 and bottom 12 and redesign them for V2 based on the feedback,
"but I don't want something that looks designed by committee, rather a
high-taste designer who sees all the feedback and is selective about which to
regard and what to do about it." He wants to see whether the redesigns result in
tangible aesthetic improvement.

## The redesigner's stance

You are one designer with strong, specific taste, not a committee secretary.
Read everything the six judges said about your scene, then decide:

- **Keep** what is genuinely the scene's soul, even if a judge disliked it.
- **Act on** the criticism that is right, in *your* way (not necessarily the
  judge's suggested fix).
- **Reject** criticism that would dilute the piece, and say why in one line.
- Add at most one or two ideas of your own if the scene needs a new idea.
- A V2 should be recognisably the same piece, only more fully itself: sharper,
  more beautiful, more alive. For the bottom-12 scenes (several are Raph's
  2016 Processing originals), a bolder rethink is allowed, but honour what the
  original was reaching for; for the top-12, restraint: don't break what works.

Coherence beats coverage: three well-chosen changes beat ten half-applied ones.

## Deliverables per scene

1. `harness/v2/<id>.md` — the redesign note, written BEFORE coding:
   - **The piece in one sentence** (what it is at its best).
   - **Feedback triage**: a table of the judges' points (who said it) → Act /
     Reject / Reinterpret, with a one-line reason each.
   - **The plan**: the 2–5 changes you will make, and what you will not touch.
   - After building: **What changed** and **Before/after** (which frames prove
     it), and anything you'd still do.
2. `web/scenes/v2/<id>.js` — the V2 scene, a NEW file (never edit the V1). It
   registers `id: '<id>v2'`, `name: '<V1 name> V2'`, the `order` given in your
   task, and a full `gallery` block (its `lineage` should name the V1 and the
   feedback you acted on). Start from a copy of the V1 source where that helps.

## The standards still apply

`harness/TASTE.md` in full: the beat visible but confined (check with
`harness/jolt.mjs`), the drop obviously bigger, movement where the subject
allows, no default rainbow/glow finish, 60 fps at full screen (judged later
with `harness/fps.mjs`, so avoid needless per-pixel cost), and no full-screen
strobing.
