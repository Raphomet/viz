# Batch 02

Ten briefs, deliberately spread wide: figurative and abstract, 2D and 3D,
calm and psychedelic. Each names a subject, its layers, and how the music
lands. They are starting points; a maker who finds something better should
follow it and say why.

Every scene must pass the "can the room see the music?" test in `TASTE.md`:
a kick punch that lands and decays, a distinct snare event, a bass swell,
hat sparkle, and an obviously bigger drop. Every scene has at least three
layers.

## 01 · Abyss (`abyss`): bioluminescent jellyfish
A deep-sea column. **Layers:** faint god rays from far above; drifting marine
snow at several depths (parallax); a handful of glowing jellyfish, bells
drawn as translucent domes with rim light and trailing tentacles (verlet
chains); tiny sparkling plankton.
**Music:** each kick contracts every bell (a visible pulse that propels it
upward); the snare flashes a wave of bioluminescence through the tentacles;
bass deepens the glow; hats sparkle the plankton. The drop brings more jellies
and colour.

## 02 · Aurora (`aurora`): northern lights over a lake
Night landscape. **Layers:** star field with a slow sky rotation; aurora
curtains (vertical ribbons of light with folds, drawn with noise); layered
mountain silhouettes with parallax; a still lake reflecting all of it, with
ripples.
**Music:** bass swells the curtains' brightness and height; the kick sends a
pulse of light racing along the curtains and a ripple across the lake; the
snare throws a shooting star; hats twinkle the stars. The drop turns the
curtains from green to green-violet-magenta and makes them dance.

## 03 · Skyline (`skyline`): fireworks over a city
A city at night from across a river. **Layers:** dusky gradient sky; fireworks
(shells launching, bursting into particles with gravity, drag, glitter and
fading smoke); the skyline silhouette with lit windows; the river reflecting
everything.
**Music:** kicks launch shells; the snare bursts them (or bursts a big one);
hats flicker the windows and add glitter; bass lifts smoke glow. The drop is a
full finale.

## 04 · Rave (`rave`): lasers over a crowd
Inside a club. **Layers:** haze with volumetric light; laser fans and beams
sweeping from the back (thin, saturated, additive); a stage wash; a crowd of
silhouetted heads and raised hands in the foreground, bobbing to the beat.
**Music:** the crowd bobs on every kick; the snare switches the laser pattern;
hats strobe fine sparkles in the haze; bass fills the haze colour. The drop
opens every laser at once and raises the hands.

## 05 · Oil (`oil`): 1960s liquid light show
The Joshua Light Show look: coloured oil and water pressed between glass on an
overhead projector. **Layers:** a dye-coloured ground; slow blobs of immiscible
colour that merge and split (metaballs or a fluid sim); thin-film iridescent
bubbles; a vignette and projector-lens bloom.
**Music:** each kick presses the glass (blobs squash and spread, then relax);
the snare drops a new drop of dye; bass drives the swirl; hats shimmer the
iridescence. Psychedelic by design.

## 06 · Mandala (`mandala`): kaleidoscope with feedback
A kaleidoscope of a living source image (your choice: flowing paint, particles,
petals) with video feedback so each frame echoes into the next. **Layers:** the
feedback trail; the mirrored source; a centre bloom; fine hat sparkle.
**Music:** kicks punch a zoom into the feedback tunnel; the snare rotates the
segment count or flips the palette; bass swells the colour saturation; the drop
adds segments and speeds the rotation. Unashamedly psychedelic, but with a
chosen palette.

## 07 · Descent (`descent`): fractal fly-through
A raymarched 3D fractal (Mandelbox, Mandelbulb, or a kaleidoscopic IFS) that
the camera flies through slowly, with fog, glow, and ambient occlusion. Keep the
raymarch cheap (low steps, low resolution, upscaled well).
**Music:** kicks pulse a glow through the structure and nudge the camera; the
snare shifts the fractal's fold parameter for a moment; bass morphs the form;
hats sparkle specular glints. The drop brightens and accelerates the flight.

## 08 · Murmuration (`murmuration`): starlings at dusk
A flock of thousands of starlings over a marsh at sunset. **Layers:** a sunset
sky gradient with clouds; the sun; the flock (boids, drawn as tiny dark
flecks whose density makes the shape); reeds in the foreground.
**Music:** the kick tightens the flock into a denser shape for a moment; the
snare makes the whole flock turn together (a visible wave through it); bass
swells the flock's size; hats shimmer the reeds. The drop makes the
murmuration surge and fold dramatically.

## 09 · Bloom (`bloom`): a night garden
Flowers that grow and open to the music. **Layers:** a dark blue night with
a moon; grass and stems growing (L-systems or procedural curves) with parallax;
flowers opening and closing; fireflies.
**Music:** each kick opens the flowers a notch (petals flare, then settle); the
snare makes a new flower bloom; bass sways the stems; hats blink the fireflies.
The drop lights the garden up with colour.

## 10 · Iris (`iris`): the psychedelic eye
A giant eye filling the stage, but surreal. **Layers:** a fractal or fibrous
iris (radial noise fibres, crypts, a limbal ring); a pupil that is a window into
another world (a starfield or tunnel); a wet specular highlight; a slow
psychedelic colour shift in the iris.
**Music:** the pupil dilates with the bass and snaps on each kick; the snare
makes the eye blink (or the iris fibres flare); hats sparkle the highlight; the
drop floods the iris with colour and opens the pupil wide. It should be
arresting, not creepy.
