## Cube (`cube`) · 2026-09-28
"A cube-shaped machine with gaskets, windows, lights, and buttons on each side.
We're looking at it from an isometric point of view. On each facet the gaskets,
windows etc. are reacting to music."

A single machine-cube seen in true isometric (three faces visible), drawn with
the precision of an industrial-design illustration or a toy: riveted panels,
rubber gaskets that compress and breathe, porthole windows with things moving
inside (gauges, spinning fans, liquid sloshing, a tiny figure at work),
indicator lamps, rows of push-buttons that depress, toggle switches, dials,
vents, a readout. Each face is its own instrument panel and each responds to a
different part of the music, so the room can read the mix on the cube: e.g.
the kick presses one big button, the snare flips a row of toggles, hats blink
lamps, bass swells the gaskets and spins the fans, the drop opens hatches and
extends things out of the cube. Movement: the cube can slowly turn a quarter at
a time (a mechanical rotation to show the next face), or it sits on a
turntable. Palette and material: choose something distinctive (enamel and
brass, pastel industrial, Braun-white with one orange), not glow.

## Iso City (`isocity`) · 2026-09-28
"Something interesting with an isometrically-drawn city."

An isometric city with real charm and density: blocks of buildings, rooftops
with water towers and gardens, streets with tiny cars and pedestrians, trains
on viaducts, a river with boats, parks, construction cranes. Make it
*interesting*, not a SimCity screenshot: the city could build itself block by
block to the music, be seen at a slowly turning angle, cycle through day and
night with windows lighting in patterns, or grow impossibly (Escher stairways,
towers stacking into the clouds). Movement: a slow isometric pan across an
endless city, or a camera that drifts along a boulevard. Music: kicks raise or
light one building, the snare sends a train across, hats flash windows, bass
sets the traffic, the drop turns the city festive or makes it grow skyward.

## Chomper (`chomper`) · 2026-09-28
"Pacman playing itself, but complexity and surrealism x100."

Our own original character and maze (Pac-Man and its ghosts are trademarked,
so no likeness, names or sprites): an arcade maze game that plays itself with
an AI, but pushed into the surreal. Ideas to escalate: mazes nested inside
pellets, the maze folding into 3D or wrapping onto a torus, the chomper
eating its way into a new maze at a different scale, pursuers that multiply
and morph into strange creatures, pellets that bloom into gardens, the whole
board tilting into perspective, time slowing when the power pellet is eaten
and the palette inverting. Keep the crisp arcade vector/pixel graphics as the
starting grammar, then break it. Music: the chomper's jaw on the kick, the snare
makes the pursuers turn, hats flicker the pellets, the drop triggers the power
pellet and the surreal escalation; the breakdown returns to a calm single maze.
