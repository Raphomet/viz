# Batch 04

Raph (2026-09-28): "we're overusing rainbows / full prism / glowy a little
too much ... Try 10 more. Go for much drastically more different this time."

So this batch deliberately leaves the dark-ground, additive-glow, spectrum
look behind. Most are daylight, flat, matte, printed, painted or made of a
material. Each is as far from the others as possible. The rules that stay:
the beat is visible but confined, the drop is obviously bigger, travel where
the subject allows. None of these may default to a rainbow palette or rely on
glow/bloom for its look. A light-ground scene may offer a dark variant as a
param, but its default is the light one.

## 01 · Poster (`poster`): Swiss kinetic typography
The International Typographic Style: a white poster, a strict grid, black and
one red, big grotesque type and bold geometric forms (circles, bars, rules)
that slide, rotate and re-set in the grid. Words about music and light, or
just letterforms and numerals. Flat, crisp, no gradients. **Music:** kicks
snap one element to a new grid position; the snare flips a block from black
to red; the drop re-sets the whole composition in a new layout (animated, not
cut) with more elements in play.

## 02 · Mobile (`mobile`): a Calder mobile
A hanging Calder-style mobile in a white gallery: flat painted metal leaves in
red, black, yellow and blue on thin black wires, balanced arms that turn and
sway with real pendulum physics, casting soft grey shadows on the wall and
floor. **Movement:** slow rotation, and the camera circles gently. **Music:**
the kick nudges one arm so it swings; the snare sets a different arm
spinning; the bass is the air current; the drop adds arms or a second mobile.

## 03 · Clay (`clay`): claymation
A plasticine world animated on twos: lumpy hand-made shapes with fingerprints
and slight irregularity, soft studio lighting and real shadows, bright
saturated but matte clay colours. A little landscape with creatures (snails,
birds, flowers, a volcano, a sun with a face), or abstract blobs that morph.
**Music:** kicks squash and stretch one creature; the snare makes something
pop out of the ground; the drop brings everyone out to dance. Rendered with
raymarched SDFs and a clay shader, or 2D with careful shading.

## 04 · Koi (`koi`): a pond from above in daylight
Looking down into a clear, shallow pond on a sunny day: pebbles and sand on
the bottom with moving sunlight caustics, koi (white, orange, black) gliding
with real body undulation, lily pads and a lotus, fallen petals drifting,
ripples, the shadows of fish on the bottom. **Movement:** the fish swim and
circle; the surface drifts. **Music:** a kick drops a petal or a raindrop at
one point, ripples spreading; the snare makes one koi dart; the bass sets how
fast the fish swim; the drop sends a shoal around the pond.

## 05 · Zen (`zen`): raked sand garden
A karesansui garden seen from above: raked gravel in parallel lines that curve
around moss-covered stones, a wooden rake drawing new lines, soft afternoon
shadows from a maple whose leaves occasionally fall. Pale gravel, grey stone,
deep green moss. **Movement:** the rake draws continuously, re-raking the
garden into new patterns over minutes. **Music:** the kick presses one ring
into the gravel around a stone; the snare drops a maple leaf; the bass sets
the rake's pace; the drop re-rakes the whole garden in a new pattern (waves,
spirals).

## 06 · Impasto (`impasto`): thick oil paint
A landscape painted live in thick, directional oil strokes in the manner of
Van Gogh's late fields: a wheat field bending in wind under a swirling sky,
cypress trees, crows. Each stroke has a palette-knife ridge and a lit edge
(impasto), laid along a flow field. Earthy ochres, blues and greens.
**Movement:** the wind rolls through the wheat and the sky swirls. **Music:**
kicks send a gust through one patch of wheat; the snare lifts a flock of crows;
the bass drives the wind; the drop brings the sun out and deepens the colour.

## 07 · Isle (`isle`): isometric toy island
A tiny low-poly island seen isometrically, like a toy diorama: a lighthouse,
windmills, a harbour with boats, a village, trees, clouds casting shadows, a
day that turns to dusk and back. Flat-shaded pastel colours, crisp edges.
**Movement:** boats sail, windmills turn, clouds drift, and the island slowly
rotates. **Music:** kicks turn one windmill a notch or ring the lighthouse bell
(a ripple of birds); the snare sends a boat out of the harbour; the bass sets
the wind; the drop lights the village with a festival.

## 08 · Pop (`pop`): comic book
Roy Lichtenstein pop art: panels of a comic page with Ben-Day dots, heavy
black outlines, flat primary colours (red, yellow, blue) on newsprint,
speech balloons and onomatopoeia. Panels change and slide along a page.
**Music:** the kick lands a "POW"-style burst in one panel only; the snare
turns the page to a new panel; the drop fills the page with action panels.
Keep text readable, witty, and about sound and light.

## 09 · Knit (`knit`): live knitting
The screen is a piece of knitted fabric (or cross-stitch on linen) being made
row by row: real stitch shapes with yarn fibres and shading, a pattern emerging
in three or four yarn colours (Fair Isle motifs, a landscape, a folk pattern).
**Movement:** the fabric scrolls upward as new rows are knitted; the pattern
evolves. **Music:** each kick knits one stitch brighter or in an accent colour
where the needle is; the snare introduces a new motif; the bass sets the
knitting speed; the drop switches to a bolder pattern with more colours.

## 10 · Plate (`plate`): a natural-history colour plate
An Ernst Haeckel plate from *Kunstformen der Natur*: radiolaria, diatoms,
jellyfish and siphonophores in intricate radial symmetry, drawn as a
chromolithograph on aged cream paper with a plate number and a Latin caption.
Muted, printed colour (ochre, sepia, slate blue, rose), fine lithographic line.
**Movement:** the organisms slowly turn and pulse; some drift across the plate.
**Music:** kicks pulse one organism; the snare rotates the plate's
arrangement; the drop fills the plate with new specimens.
