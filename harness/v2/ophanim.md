# Ophanim — V2

## The piece in one sentence

The angel of Ezekiel as the still point of a deep stack of moving planes: a
starfield streaming past with split-colour trails, halos and wheels of eyes
flying in out of the depth and passing *around* the being, and at the centre
the wheels themselves melting from circle to lens to rosette while six
translucent wings fold into a shell and burst open on the drop.

## Scope: a restaging around layers, keeping the angel's anatomy

This V2 is not driven by the panel alone. Raph asked for it directly after
studying LEXSAN's set (`docs/research/2026-09-28-lexsan-takeaways.md`), about
#13, Psychedelic mirror shards: "the way the shapes are morphing is super
interesting and mesmerizing. also good use of layers with the rings flying in,
the glow, and the starfield background. if we applied some of these layerings
to our angel it would be amazing to behold. great palette too."

So the scope is **layering, morphing and palette**, and the angel's anatomy
stays: the gimbal of wheels in true perspective with eyes that all turn to
look at you, six wings, the fire at the centre, rays confined to the being,
and V1's music vocabulary (kick ratchets a wheel, snare beats the wings, hats
throw lightning, bass swells the fire). What changes is everything around and
through it, and the surface: V1's opaque gold leaf becomes flat translucent
inks that mix where they overlap (#13's pink over cyan making lilac), with
the gold kept as the thread back to V1 in the rims, axles and eyelids.

## The plan

1. **The stack**, back to front, all in one perspective camera:
   - *Deep ground and starfield*: a violet-black ground in a round porthole
     vignette (from #13), and ~340 stars flying toward the viewer. Their
     trails are drawn three times, offset radially in red, green and blue and
     added, so a streak splits into colour at its ends (#13's chromatic
     streaks). Speed follows the section: drifting points in the breakdown,
     warp streaks in the drop.
   - *Flying rings, back half*: halos of eyes, mandorlas and spoked wheels in
     translucent ink with gold wire, tilted and spinning, arriving out of the
     depth. Each is split at the angel's depth, so its far side passes
     behind the being and its near side in front: they visibly go *around*
     it. Calm: one every few seconds. Drop: every kick launches one.
   - *The angel*: nested translucent mandorla bands (whose overlaps stack to
     a saturated centre), rays, six translucent wings, the gimbal of wheels
     with eyes, the fire.
   - *Flying rings, front half*, then lightning, sparks, glints and gold dust
     in the foreground, moving fastest with the camera.
2. **Morphing forms.** Each wheel's profile is a continuous family: circle →
   pointed lens (a vesica, like the mandorla) → lobed rosette (3–6 lobes),
   with its band width breathing, driven by its own slow clocks and deepened
   by the drop. The mandorla behind breathes between a vesica and a round
   nimbus. The wings' feathers carry a travelling length wave, and the whole
   wing set morphs between spread and a closed shell.
3. **Palette.** Three outfits of four inks each on a deep ground, mixing by
   plain translucency. Default: Rose window (pink, cyan, saffron, violet on
   violet-black). The "Cycle" setting melts to the next outfit during each
   breakdown.
4. **Performer model.** Palette, Wheels, Morph, Flight, Glory, Reaction,
   Wings, Follow the track, with `presets: { calm, drop }`.

Not touched: the gimbal construction, the eyes (V1's icon roundels, which
foreshorten with the rim and turn to look at you), the wing geometry, the
fire's petal crowns, the music detection. The signature-eye project's styles
were looked at and not borrowed: they are designed as heroes, and here the
eyes are many and small.

## What changed

- **Five planes in one camera.** Ground and porthole; ~420 stars flying at
  the lens with RGB-split trails (points in the breakdown, warp streaks in the
  drop); halos of eyes, spoked wheels and mandorlas flying in and split at the
  angel's depth so they pass around it; the angel; lightning, sparks, glints
  and dust in front. The camera drifts (±0.34 units), banks (±2.6°) and
  dollies in about 10% on the drop, so every plane parallaxes differently.
- **Flat translucent inks.** Wheels, wings, mandorla bands and flying rings
  are translucent fills with gold wire; overlaps mix (cyan wing over pink
  wing is lilac, pink wheel over saffron ring is coral). Eyes stay opaque and
  luminous. The centre's radiance is six nested translucent vesicas plus
  rays in pink and cyan and a thin nimbus.
- **Morphing.** Each wheel's profile runs on its own clocks through circle,
  pointed lens and 3–6-lobed rosette, with its band width breathing; the eyes
  ride the changing rim. The mandorla breathes between vesica and round; the
  flying rings morph on their own clocks; a length wave runs along the
  feathers.
- **The coil.** Rising hats fold the wings: first upswept into a V, then a
  closed shell (upper pair arched over, lower pair under, flying pair standing
  along the sides). The drop throws them wide within about a beat.
- **Palette per drop.** Rose window, Ember, Lapis and gold, melted in OKLab
  over five seconds during each breakdown ("New outfit each breakdown" is the
  default; any one outfit can be pinned).
- **Kick in the drop launches a ring** out of the depth (it surfaces around
  the angel a second or two later), on top of V1's ratchet and eye flare.
- **Second pass (coordinator's review): the wheels snap on the beat.** Each
  kick sends the wheel it ratchets to its next shape key (circle, lens,
  rosette, lens-rosette) through an underdamped spring (13 rad/s, damping
  0.3, about a third overshoot, settled in half a second), like Solids'
  sproing. The slow drift remains underneath at a smaller amplitude. In the
  drop each wheel snaps about every two seconds, round-robin.
- **Second pass: the drop's centre holds its colour.** The fire's heart is
  now painted (a small white-gold point over a rose-gold core) rather than
  added, the rays start outside the heart, and the scene asks the Finish for
  three-fifths bloom (`finish: { bloom: 0.6 }`). The peak reads as saturated
  rose and gold with the wheels' colours intact (12 s and 14 s at 720),
  where the first pass summed to white.
- Params: Palette, Wheels, Morph, Flight, Glory, Reaction strength, Wings,
  Follow the track; `presets: { calm, drop }` (Follow eases Morph, Flight and
  Glory to the drop look while the drop is detected).

## What I'd still do

- The angel is still central. The camera drift moves it at most about 75 px on the 600 short side;
  a larger orbit on later drops would answer the Curator further.
- #13's left-right mirror was not used: the angel is already bilateral. A
  mirrored shard layer behind it is the next idea if the rings ever feel thin.
